6. Shell rules the build follows

  • Sidebar: drag the edge 1:1 to resize (200 to 360 px), fold to 72 px icons, the folded edge opens on drag or click, size remembered in localStorage before first paint.
  • Tabs: under the page header on any screen with 3 or more sections; all sections rendered once and hidden; tab in the address; bar scrolls inside itself on phones.
  • Phone: docked 5-tab bar (Today, Orbit, Studio, Plan, More) with safe-area padding; sidebar becomes a sheet; fields 16 px; no pinch zoom; dvh heights.
  • Desktop: page max 1320 px with a 3% gutter; rows and tables, grids that add columns; never a narrow centred column.
  • Shapes: buttons one rounded rectangle (14 px); pills only for labels and counts; cards 20 px with glass.
  • Words: never "client" on a screen; a pasted link always shows its type icon; prices in rupees, GST stated.
  • Speed: skeleton under 100 ms on tap, one database trip per page, 30 s cache with live refresh, no polling.
  • Pictures: every upload and avatar from media.nevorai.com (R2); no paid image optimiser.
